## What Happens When You Import

1. Click "+ Import Video"
2. Select your video file
3. FFmpeg extracts:
   - Duration
   - Resolution  
   - File size
   - Video format
4. Thumbnail is generated at 1 second mark
5. Clip appears in Media Library with thumbnail
6. Clip appears on Timeline
7. Click play to preview the video!
